# Taste bar

## The bar

- Content first. Space and type do the work.
- One narrow palette. One accent at most.
- Real imagery or none. No decorative illustration.
- Motion only when it explains a change.
- A voice that fits the product, not a borrowed trend.
- No category template: no logo wall, no giant metrics, no identical card grid,
  no badge above the headline, unless the content truly needs them.
- No house style. The references are calibration, not templates.

## Good calibration

- **JJJJound:** product first. Black and white, large photographs, small
  uppercase labels, wide space, almost no chrome. The brand is present but never
  competes with the product.
- **Nous:** technical and editorial. Strong type, a serif and mono mix, dark and
  cream surfaces, a real voice. The structure stays plain and readable.

Take the principle: restraint, content first, a clear voice. Do not take the
palette, the type, or the layout. A clone of either is a house style.

## Bad calibration

- **Ponytail and Kilo:** the AI-tool landing-page pattern. Oversized italic
  serif, giant metrics, logo walls, identical feature cards, badges, gradients,
  motion on everything, and claims like "built differently".
- **The Impeccable slop catalog** (`https://impeccable.style/slop/`): purple
  gradients, glass and glow, cardocalypse, Inter everywhere, icon tiles, a badge
  above the headline, pulsing dots, auto-scrolling logos.

## The slop list

Do not use these as decoration:

- purple-to-blue gradients, gradient text, glow, glass panels, orbs
- nested cards, thick side-tab borders, huge corner radii, broad shadows
- identical icon-heading-text card grids
- a small label or badge above the headline
- oversized italic serif as a shortcut to "editorial"
- Inter or Geist everywhere with no reason
- pulsing status dots, blinking cursors, auto-scrolling marquees
- bounce or elastic easing
- placeholder illustrations, emoji as icons
- generic claims: "supercharge", "world-class", "not a feature, a platform"

## Use the bar

Before a direction, ask: does this come from the product, or from a template?
If the answer is a template, delete it and start from the product facts.
