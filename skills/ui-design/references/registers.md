# Registers

The register is the kind of surface. It sets the ambition level. Choose it from
the requested surface, not the product as a whole.

## Operate

The visitor completes a task. App UI, dashboards, editors, admin, settings,
tools.

- **Success:** the task is done, quickly and without doubt.
- **Ambition:** precision, density, familiar patterns, clear states.
- **Avoid:** marketing heroes, oversized type, decorative motion, logo walls.
- **Test:** can a person find the main action and finish it?

## Persuade

The visitor decides and acts. Landing pages, marketing, campaigns, pricing.

- **Success:** the visitor understands the offer and takes the action.
- **Ambition:** a point of view, a story, real imagery, one clear action.
- **Avoid:** generic claims, logo walls, identical feature cards, motion with no
  job.
- **Test:** does the page show the product and the next step?

## Read

The visitor understands something. Docs, articles, guides, changelogs.

- **Success:** the reader finds and understands the answer.
- **Ambition:** structure, measure, type, reading comfort.
- **Avoid:** decoration that competes with the text, tiny body text, long lines.
- **Test:** can a person read a long page without strain?

## Experience

The visitor is inside the work. Portfolios, galleries, showcases.

- **Success:** the work leads; the interface recedes.
- **Ambition:** the artifact is the first viewport; chrome is minimal.
- **Avoid:** interface elements that compete with the work.
- **Test:** does the work get the attention, not the frame?

## Mixed products

A product can have a Persuade landing page, an Operate app and a Read docs
section. Record each surface in `DESIGN.md` with its own register and intent.
Share the foundation.
